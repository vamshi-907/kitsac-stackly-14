7 Everyday Habits That Support Your Immunity

Dr. Rhea June 12, 2026 6 min read

Your immune system isn't a switch you flip — it's a chorus of small, daily habits. As pharmacists, we hear the same questions again and again: "What actually works?" Here's the honest, evidence-based short list we share at the counter.

1. Sleep like you mean it

Adults need 7–9 hours. If you're consistently under that, it's the highest-leverage change you can make this week.

2. Move most days

Twenty to thirty minutes of moderate activity is enough to move the needle. Walk after meals.

3. Prioritize protein and produce

Half your plate should be colorful. Protein at each meal helps keep energy stable.

4. Manage stress like a project

Even 10 minutes of stillness or breathwork lowers cortisol enough to matter.

5. Stay hydrated

Two liters is a reasonable default; more if you exercise or live somewhere hot.

6. Consider a daily multivitamin

Not a magic bullet — a safety net. Ask us which one fits your labs and diet.

7. Ask questions

Pop into the pharmacy. Free advice, no appointment. That's what we're here for.
